04 / HOW MAGE WORKSLearning in the external state
A PERSISTENT LEARNING LOOP
Keep the weights.
Build the experience.
During evolution, guidance writes corrections and principles. The learner’s own successful answers become reusable examples. At inference, the frozen learner consults task-conditioned memories.
01
Choose what to learn
Capability structure and curriculum selection direct attention toward skills and observed task types.
02
Record both outcomes
Harvest correct traces from the learner and add teacher-written corrections for observed failures.
03
Retrieve and reuse
Task-filtered success and failure memories guide the frozen learner. Search and routing bandits adapt during evolution.
